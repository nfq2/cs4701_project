A quick guide to Leduc

The deck has two Jacks, two Queens, and two Kings. You and the agent each get one private card. Bet once, then a shared card is revealed for a second betting round. A pair with the shared card wins; otherwise, the higher private rank wins. Equal ranks split the pot.

Check passes when no chips are owed. Call matches the bet. Raise increases it. Fold gives up the hand. Blinds are 1 and 2 chips; raises are 2 chips in round 1 and 4 in round 2, with two raises allowed per round. Your score is net chips won, not a limited bankroll.
